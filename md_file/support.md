# E-Forward Support

E-Forward is a document approval and electronic signature application by Ardent Networks Inc., for authorized internal users.

## Contact us

Email: support@ardentnetworks.com.ph

We aim to respond within 1–2 business days.

## Frequently asked

**How do I get an account?**
Accounts are provisioned by your organization administrator. Contact your IT/admin team to be granted access.

**I forgot my password.**
Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login screen to receive a reset email.

**How is my data handled?**
See our [Privacy Policy](https://eforward.ardentnetworks.com.ph/privacy).
